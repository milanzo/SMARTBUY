GTA 6 for PS5: What to Know Before Buying Grand Theft Auto VI

Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the most anticipated games of 2026. Here's what PS5 players should know before pre-ordering or buying GTA 6.

What Is GTA 6?

Grand Theft Auto VI is the next major entry in Rockstar Games' Grand Theft Auto series. The game takes players back to modern-day Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.

The story follows Jason and Lucia as they become caught up in a criminal conspiracy after an easy score goes wrong.

Rockstar has described GTA VI as a major evolution of the Grand Theft Auto experience, with the game built for the current generation of consoles.

GTA 6 Release Date

November 19, 2026

Rockstar Games currently lists November 19, 2026 as the release date for Grand Theft Auto VI.

The game is scheduled for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar also lists PlayStation 5 under "Plays Best On."
