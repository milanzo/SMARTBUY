10 Best Home Organization Products That Can Help Save Space

Discover practical home organization products that can help you make better use of the space you already have.

1. Vacuum Storage Bags

Vacuum storage bags can be useful for storing clothing, bedding, blankets and other soft items.

By removing air from the bags, they can make bulky soft items easier to store. They're particularly useful when seasonal clothing or bedding needs to be put away.

2. Under-Bed Storage Containers

The space underneath a bed can be useful for storing items that aren't needed every day.

Under-bed containers can be suitable for shoes, seasonal clothing, bedding and other belongings.

When choosing one, consider the available space underneath your bed, the container dimensions and whether it has wheels or handles.

3. Drawer Organizers

Small items can quickly turn a drawer into a mess.

Drawer organizers can separate things such as accessories, stationery, cosmetics, kitchen utensils or other small household items.

Look for adjustable compartments if you want flexibility.

4. Closet Storage Organizers

Closets often have unused vertical space.

Hanging organizers, shelf dividers and additional storage compartments can help you make better use of that space without completely redesigning your closet.

Before buying, measure your closet and check the dimensions of the organizer.

5. Stackable Storage Bins

Stackable bins can help you use vertical space efficiently.

They're useful for closets, bedrooms, garages, utility rooms and other areas where floor space is limited.

Clear bins can also make it easier to see what's inside without opening every container.

6. Shoe Organizers

Shoes can take up considerable floor space when they're left scattered around a room.

A shoe rack, hanging shoe organizer or stackable shoe storage system can keep footwear together and make it easier to find.

Choose the type based on the amount of space available and the number of pairs you need to store.

7. Storage Baskets

Storage baskets are a simple way to organize everyday items while keeping them out of sight.

They can be useful in bedrooms, bathrooms, living rooms and offices.

Consider baskets with handles if you expect to move them frequently.

8. Cable Organizers

Chargers, USB cables and other electronic accessories can quickly become tangled.

Cable clips, cable ties and cable-management boxes can help keep charging areas and desks more organized.

They're inexpensive and can be particularly useful if several people share the same charging area.

9. Over-the-Door Organizers

Doors can provide additional storage space that is easy to overlook.

Over-the-door organizers can be useful for accessories, cleaning supplies, shoes, toiletries and other lightweight items.

Check the door thickness and the organizer's dimensions before buying.

10. Labeling Supplies

Labels may seem simple, but they can make an organization system easier to maintain.

Labels can help identify storage bins, boxes, drawers and containers, making it easier to find things later.

You can use printed labels, adhesive labels or a small label maker depending on your needs.

How to Choose Home Organization Products

Measure Your Space

Measure the area where the product will be used before ordering. A storage solution is only useful if it actually fits.

Think About What You're Storing

Different items require different types of storage. Clothing, shoes, cables and kitchen items may each benefit from different organizers.

Don't Overbuy

It's easy to purchase lots of storage containers without first deciding what needs to be organized.

Start with the areas that cause the most clutter.

Consider Accessibility

Items you use frequently should be easy to reach. Seasonal belongings can be placed in less accessible storage areas.

Check Product Specifications

Always check dimensions, materials, weight limits and compatibility with your available space before purchasing.

SmartBuy Organization Tip: Before buying storage products, sort your belongings first. Separate items into categories such as keep, donate, recycle and discard. Then choose storage products based on what you actually need to organize.

Final Thoughts

A well-organized home doesn't have to require expensive furniture or major renovations.

Simple products such as vacuum storage bags, drawer organizers, storage bins, shoe organizers and cable-management accessories can help make better use of the space you already have.

The best organization products are the ones that fit your space, suit the items you're storing and make those items easier to access when you need them.
