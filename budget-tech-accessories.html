10 Best Budget Tech Accessories for Everyday Use

Useful technology accessories don't always have to be expensive. Here are 10 practical tech accessories worth considering for everyday work, entertainment and home use.

1. Wireless Earbuds

Wireless earbuds are useful for music, podcasts, calls and watching videos without dealing with cables.

When choosing wireless earbuds, consider comfort, battery life, connectivity, microphone quality and charging options.

2. Wireless Mouse

A wireless mouse can make using a laptop or desktop computer more comfortable, particularly if you spend a lot of time working or studying.

Look for comfortable ergonomics, reliable wireless connectivity and a battery system that suits your usage.

3. USB Charging Cable

A good charging cable is one of those accessories most people use every day. Having a spare can also be useful for work, travel or keeping in another room.

Before buying, check the connector type and whether the cable supports the charging and data-transfer requirements of your device.

4. Power Bank

A power bank can be useful when you're away from a wall socket.

When comparing power banks, consider capacity, charging speed, number of ports, size and weight. A larger capacity isn't always better if portability is important to you.

5. Phone Stand

A simple phone stand can be useful for video calls, watching videos, following recipes or keeping your phone visible while working.

Look for a stable base, adjustable viewing angles and compatibility with your phone and case.

6. Bluetooth Speaker

A compact Bluetooth speaker can provide an easy way to listen to music, podcasts or other audio around the home.

Important features to compare include sound quality, battery life, portability, Bluetooth connectivity and water resistance if you plan to use the speaker outdoors.

7. Keyboard

If you regularly work at a desk, a comfortable keyboard can improve your everyday computer setup.

Consider the keyboard layout, key feel, size, connection type and whether you need additional features such as a numeric keypad or backlighting.

8. USB Hub

Modern laptops often have fewer ports than older computers. A USB hub can provide additional connections for accessories such as mice, keyboards, USB drives and other peripherals.

Before buying, check which ports you actually need. USB-A, USB-C, HDMI, Ethernet and SD-card connections are common options.

9. Cable Management Accessories

Cable clips, ties and organisers are inexpensive accessories that can make a desk much tidier.

They're particularly useful if you have several charging cables, computer accessories and other electronics in the same workspace.

10. Extension Cable Reel

An extension cable reel can be useful for DIY tasks, workshops and situations where you need additional cable reach.

How to Choose the Right Tech Accessories

Before buying any technology accessory, consider these five things:

  • Compatibility: Make sure the accessory works with your existing phone, computer, tablet or other equipment.
  • Your actual needs: Don't pay for features you'll never use.
  • Reviews and specifications: Check specifications and customer feedback before making a purchase.
  • Price: Compare prices rather than automatically choosing the cheapest option.
  • Reliability: For accessories you use every day, reliability can be more important than having the most features.
SmartBuy Tip: Before buying any product, compare the current price, specifications, customer reviews and compatibility with your devices.

Final Thoughts

You don't need to spend a fortune to improve your everyday technology setup. Small accessories such as wireless earbuds, a wireless mouse, charging equipment, phone stands and cable-management products can make everyday tasks more convenient.

The key is to identify what you actually need, compare the important specifications and choose products that offer good value for your particular use.
