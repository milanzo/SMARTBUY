Blackview Active 12 Pro Review: Is This Rugged 5G Tablet Worth Considering?

The Blackview Active 12 Pro combines rugged protection with 5G connectivity, a large battery, a high-refresh-rate display and an unusual built-in projector. Here's what to know before buying.

What Is the Blackview Active 12 Pro?

The Blackview Active 12 Pro is a rugged Android tablet designed for users who want more durability and battery capacity than a typical everyday tablet. It combines an 11-inch display with 5G connectivity, a MediaTek Dimensity 7300 processor and a large 30,000mAh battery.

One of its most unusual features is a built-in projector. Blackview says the projector can produce a 120-inch image and supports 1080p resolution, making the tablet different from many conventional rugged tablets.

Rugged Design

Durability is one of the main reasons to consider the Active 12 Pro instead of a conventional tablet. Blackview lists IP68/IP69K protection and MIL-STD-810H certification for the device.

The company also describes the tablet as being designed for demanding environments, with features such as glove mode and resistance to drops, water, dust and other challenging conditions.

SmartBuy Tip: Rugged protection does not mean a device should be deliberately abused. Always follow the manufacturer's safety and usage instructions, particularly around water, charging and extreme conditions.

A Huge 30,000mAh Battery

The Active 12 Pro has a 30,000mAh battery, which is substantially larger than the batteries found in many conventional tablets.

Blackview also lists 120W fast charging. The large battery could make the tablet particularly interesting for people who spend long periods away from a power outlet, although actual battery life will vary depending on brightness, network connection, applications and usage.

Built-In Projector

One of the most unusual features is the integrated projector. Blackview lists a 1080p projector capable of projecting an image up to 120 inches.

This could be useful for presentations, entertainment or displaying content in locations where carrying a separate projector would be inconvenient. However, projector performance will depend on the surrounding environment, projection distance and available surface.

Performance and 5G Connectivity

The tablet uses MediaTek's Dimensity 7300 processor and supports 5G. Blackview also lists Wi-Fi 6E and Bluetooth 5.4 connectivity.

For users who need a tablet for multitasking, media consumption, field work or demanding applications, the hardware makes the Active 12 Pro an interesting option to investigate.

Display and Cameras

The Active 12 Pro has an 11-inch FHD+ IPS display with a 90Hz refresh rate. Blackview lists a resolution of 1920 × 1200 and a screen-to-body ratio of 83 percent.

For photography and video calls, the tablet includes a 108MP Samsung HM6 rear camera and a 50MP Samsung JN1 front camera.

Who Is the Blackview Active 12 Pro For?

The Active 12 Pro may appeal to people who need a rugged tablet for demanding environments, outdoor activities, travel, field work or situations where a large battery and durable construction are important.

It may also appeal to users who simply want a powerful tablet with unusual features such as an integrated projector and very large battery.

Who Might Want a Different Tablet?

If your priority is a lightweight tablet for reading, casual browsing or carrying around all day, the Active 12 Pro may be more tablet than you need. Its rugged construction and large battery add considerable weight and bulk.

For users who rarely need rugged protection, a conventional tablet could be a simpler and lighter alternative.

Things to Check Before Buying

  • Configuration: Check whether you're buying the 12GB/256GB or 16GB/1TB version.
  • Network compatibility: If you intend to use 5G, check that the supported bands work with your mobile network.
  • Weight: At around 1,522g, this is a heavy tablet compared with conventional models.
  • Projector: Consider whether the built-in projector is genuinely useful for your needs.
  • Storage: Check the included storage and whether you need additional expandable storage.
  • Price: Amazon prices and availability can change, so check the current listing before purchasing.

Our Verdict

The Blackview Active 12 Pro is an unusual rugged tablet that combines durability, 5G connectivity, a large 30,000mAh battery and a built-in projector in one device.

Its size and weight mean it won't be the right choice for everyone, but if you need a rugged tablet with substantial battery capacity and more features than a typical tablet, it is certainly worth investigating.
