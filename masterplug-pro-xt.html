Where Could You Use It?

A cable reel can be useful when working in areas where a nearby socket is too far away. Possible applications include DIY projects, workshops, gardens and other temporary electrical setups where an extension cable is appropriate.

The reel format can also make it easier to wind the cable away after use instead of leaving a long loose cable on the floor.

Electrical Safety: Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and the safety guidance supplied with the product. Do not exceed the reel's rated electrical load. Extension reels should be used and stored safely, and cables should be fully unwound when required by the manufacturer's instructions, particularly when powering higher loads.
