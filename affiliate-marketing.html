Affiliate marketing is one of the ways people can build an online business without creating their own products.

Instead of developing a product yourself, you recommend products or services created by other businesses. When someone purchases through your unique affiliate link, you may receive a commission.

What Is Affiliate Marketing?

The basic idea is simple:

Find a useful product → recommend it → someone clicks your affiliate link → they purchase → you earn a commission.

However, successful affiliate marketing involves much more than simply posting links. You need to understand your audience, choose suitable products and create useful content that people actually want to read or watch.

Can Beginners Start Affiliate Marketing?

Yes. You don't necessarily need to create your own product, hold inventory or operate a physical shop.

Beginners can use platforms such as websites, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest and other channels to attract people who are interested in particular products or topics.
