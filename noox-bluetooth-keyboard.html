Why Consider a Bluetooth Keyboard for a Tablet?

Touchscreens are convenient, but typing long messages, emails, documents or notes can become uncomfortable. A physical keyboard can make longer typing sessions feel more familiar.

A compact Bluetooth keyboard can also turn a compatible tablet into a more laptop-like setup without requiring a permanent wired connection.

Portable Design

The slim design of the NOOX keyboard makes it suitable for people who want to carry a keyboard with their tablet or laptop bag.

Its compact form can make it useful for travelling, studying, working from different locations or simply keeping a keyboard available when you need one.

Bluetooth Connectivity

The keyboard uses Bluetooth connectivity rather than requiring a USB receiver. This can be convenient for tablets and smartphones that may not have a traditional USB port available.

Compatibility

The NOOX keyboard is marketed for use with iOS, Android and Windows devices. This makes it potentially useful for people who use more than one type of device.
