JBL Wave Buds: Quick Overview

The JBL Wave Buds are true wireless in-ear earbuds designed for everyday listening, calls and portable entertainment. They feature JBL's Deep Bass sound, an ergonomic bud design, Bluetooth connectivity and compatibility with the JBL Headphones app.

According to JBL's official specifications, the earbuds use 8 mm drivers and offer up to 32 hours of total battery life, including the charging case. 1

🔊 Sound Quality

The JBL Wave Buds use 8 mm dynamic drivers and JBL Deep Bass Sound. This makes them particularly interesting for listeners who enjoy bass-heavy music and everyday entertainment. 3

The earbuds are designed to create a secure fit that helps seal the ear, which can also help with the perceived bass response. 4

SmartBuy view: If you enjoy music with noticeable bass and want a simple everyday pair of wireless earbuds, the Wave Buds are worth considering.

🔋 Battery Life

Battery life is one of the stronger features of the JBL Wave Buds. JBL rates the earbuds for up to 8 hours of playback, with another 24 hours available from the charging case, giving up to 32 hours in total. 5

JBL also says a 10-minute speed charge can provide up to two additional hours of playback. 6

💧 Water and Dust Resistance

The earbuds have an IP54 rating, while the charging case is rated IPX2. JBL describes the earbuds as water and dust resistant. 7

That can make them useful for everyday activities and outdoor use, but water resistance does not mean the earbuds are waterproof. You should still follow JBL's care instructions.

📞 Calls and Voice Features

The Wave Buds include a built-in microphone and hands-free calling. They also feature JBL VoiceAware, which lets you control how much of your own voice you hear during calls. 8

🌎 Ambient Aware and TalkThru

JBL includes Smart Ambient features designed to help you remain aware of your surroundings without completely removing the earbuds. TalkThru can also make it easier to hear people around you. 9

📱 JBL Headphones App

The JBL Wave Buds are compatible with the JBL Headphones app, which allows users to adjust aspects of their listening experience. 10

Who Are the JBL Wave Buds For?

The JBL Wave Buds may be a good option for people looking for straightforward true wireless earbuds for music, podcasts, calls and everyday listening.

They may especially appeal to listeners who like bass-focused sound and want long total battery life without moving into a premium-priced flagship category.

Are the JBL Wave Buds Worth Considering?

On paper, the JBL Wave Buds offer a useful combination of features: Deep Bass sound, up to 32 hours of total battery life, water and dust resistance, Bluetooth 5.2, hands-free calling and JBL app support. 11

Whether they're the right choice depends on your budget, preferred sound, fit and the features you need.
