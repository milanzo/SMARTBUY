Why Use Vacuum Storage Bags?

Clothes, duvets, blankets and pillows can take up a surprising amount of storage space. Vacuum storage bags are designed to compress soft items so they can be stored more compactly.

They can be particularly useful when changing seasonal clothing, organising cupboards, preparing for a move or storing spare bedding.

How to Get the Best Results

Start by choosing a bag that is large enough for the items you want to store. Avoid stuffing the bag so full that the seal cannot close properly.

After placing the items inside, close the seal carefully and remove the air according to the product's instructions. Check the seal before putting the compressed bag away.

SmartBuy Tip: Pay attention to the different bag sizes in the pack. Jumbo bags can be useful for larger bedding items, while smaller bags may be more convenient for clothing and smaller textiles.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

  • Bag sizes: Make sure the combination includes sizes suitable for your items.
  • Quantity: Consider how many bags you actually need for your storage project.
  • Seal system: Check how the bags are sealed and how air is removed.
  • Storage space: Measure your cupboard, drawer or storage area before buying.
  • Current price: Amazon prices and availability can change, so check the current listing before purchasing.
