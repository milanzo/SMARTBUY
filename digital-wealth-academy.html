Digital Wealth Academy Review: What Does DWA Teach?

An overview of Digital Wealth Academy, its training, features and what potential buyers should consider.

Digital Wealth Academy (DWA) is an online digital-marketing training program aimed at people interested in learning about digital marketing and building an online business.

The program covers several areas of online marketing, including social media, email marketing, sales funnels, affiliate marketing and digital products.

What Is Digital Wealth Academy?

DWA is designed as a broad digital-marketing training program rather than a course focused on just one online-business skill.

The sales page currently describes the program as containing more than 80 training videos covering different aspects of digital marketing.

What Does DWA Cover?

1. Digital Marketing

The training focuses on different digital-marketing concepts that can be used to promote products, services and online businesses.

2. Social Media Marketing

Social media is a major part of modern online marketing. DWA includes training around platforms such as Instagram and TikTok and discusses creating content to attract an audience.

3. Email Marketing

Email marketing can help businesses communicate with people who have already shown interest in their content or products.

DWA includes training around email marketing and building an audience that can be reached through email.

4. Sales Funnels

A sales funnel is a sequence of steps designed to move a potential customer from discovering a product to considering and purchasing it.

DWA includes training around sales funnels and how they can be used in online marketing.
