Best Water Filter Cartridges for Home Use: BRITA MAXTRA PRO Guide

Looking for replacement water filter cartridges? Here's what to consider when choosing a BRITA MAXTRA PRO filter for your home.

Why Use a Water Filter Cartridge?

Many people use water filters because they want to improve the taste and smell of their tap water or reduce certain substances present in mains water.

BRITA says its MAXTRA PRO filters can reduce substances including chlorine and certain metals such as lead and copper. The exact filtration performance depends on the cartridge type and local water conditions.

Which MAXTRA PRO Cartridge Should You Choose?

BRITA offers different MAXTRA PRO cartridge options. The right choice depends partly on your local water hardness and what you want from the filtration.

MAXTRA PRO PURE PERFORMANCE

BRITA describes PURE PERFORMANCE as an option for soft to medium water areas. It is designed to reduce taste-impairing substances such as chlorine and also addresses certain trace impurities.

MAXTRA PRO LIMESCALE EXPERT

LIMESCALE EXPERT is designed for hard to very hard water areas and places greater emphasis on limescale reduction and appliance protection.

How Often Should You Replace the Filter?

BRITA recommends replacing a MAXTRA PRO cartridge at least every four weeks. The maximum stated service life is 150 litres, but the actual useful life can be affected by local water hardness and how much water you filter.

SmartBuy Tip: Don't assume that reaching 150 litres means you should continue using the cartridge beyond four weeks. BRITA specifically recommends replacement at least every four weeks.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

  • Compatibility: Make sure the cartridge is suitable for your BRITA jug.
  • Water hardness: Consider whether your area has soft, medium or hard water.
  • Cartridge type: Choose the MAXTRA PRO variant that matches your needs.
  • Pack size: Buying multiple cartridges can be convenient if you use filtered water regularly.
  • Current price: Compare the current price before purchasing because Amazon prices and promotions can change.

Our Verdict

The BRITA MAXTRA PRO range is worth considering if you already use a compatible BRITA water-filter jug and need replacement cartridges. The 150-litre maximum capacity and four-week replacement guidance make the replacement schedule relatively straightforward.

The most important thing is choosing the cartridge type that suits your local water conditions and checking that it is compatible with your particular BRITA system.
