Logitech M185 Review

The Logitech M185 is designed for people who want a straightforward wireless mouse without complicated setup. Its compact design makes it particularly suitable for laptops, home offices and everyday computer use.

The mouse uses a 2.4 GHz wireless connection through its small USB receiver. Logitech's specifications list a 1000 DPI optical tracking system and a replaceable AA battery with a rated battery life of up to 12 months. 1
