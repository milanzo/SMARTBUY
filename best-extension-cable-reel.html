Why Use an Extension Cable Reel?

An extension cable reel can be useful when the nearest power outlet is some distance away from where you're working. A reel also gives you a convenient way to store a long extension cable when it isn't being used.

Cable reels can be particularly useful for suitable DIY projects, workshops, gardens and other situations where additional cable reach is required.

⚠️ Important safety information: Never exceed the cable reel's rated load. Cable reels can have different power limits depending on whether the cable is fully unwound or still wound on the reel. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and safety guidance. The Pro-XT documentation identifies a thermal cut-out designed to disconnect power if the cable overheats.

Fully Unwind the Cable When Required

With cable reels, it is important to pay attention to the manufacturer's instructions about using the cable wound or unwound. Electrical cable can generate heat when carrying current, and the amount of cable left on the reel can affect the permitted load.

For the safest use, check the product instructions and make sure the cable is deployed as recommended for the load you are powering.

Is It Suitable for EV Charging?

No. You should not assume that a standard extension cable reel is suitable for electric-vehicle charging. Masterplug Pro-XT products and comparable cable reels can have specific restrictions around EV charging. Check the exact product documentation before using any extension equipment with an EV.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

  • Cable length: Choose a length that gives you enough reach without creating unnecessary cable to store.
  • Number of sockets: Consider how many suitable devices you may need to connect.
  • Power rating: Always check the rated load and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
  • Safety features: A thermal cut-out and power indicator can be useful features.
  • Intended location: Check whether the particular model is rated for the environment where you plan to use it.
SmartBuy Tip: Don't choose an extension reel based only on cable length. Check the current rating, power limits, socket configuration and intended use before buying.

Our Verdict

The Masterplug Pro-XT is worth considering if you need a long extension cable for suitable DIY, home or workshop applications. The 25 metre cable and four sockets provide useful reach and flexibility, while the power indicator and thermal cut-out add practical safety features.

The key is using the reel within its rated limits and following the manufacturer's instructions. If you only need a short amount of additional reach, a smaller extension lead may be more convenient.
